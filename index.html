Our Story

A Legacy of Learning, A Vision for Tomorrow.

Ali Allana English Medium School is part of Jamia Islamia Ishaatul Uloom, Akkalkuwa — a trust that has served education for close to half a century.

We are an English-medium, co-educational school for Grades 1 to 10, where enduring values and sound learning grow together.

  1. The Trust

    Jamia Islamia Ishaatul Uloom has served education in Akkalkuwa for close to half a century — and now extends that service to quality school education.

  2. The School

    English medium and co-educational from Grade 1 to Std 10, with hostels for boys and girls from Std 6.

  3. The Road Ahead

    Our primary grades (1–5) are in candidacy for the IB Primary Years Programme — AAEMS is a Candidate School* for the PYP. Grades 6–10 follow the Maharashtra State Board.

IB PYP Candidate School*

How admission works

Admission to Grades 1–10 is by entrance examination. When admissions open, the process follows four steps.

  1. 1

    Enquire & Visit

    Call or email the school office. We encourage every family to visit the campus with their child before applying.

  2. 2

    Register

    Complete the registration form and pay the registration fee.

  3. 3

    Entrance Examination

    Your child sits the entrance examination on the announced date.

  4. 4

    Admission & Documents

    If a place is offered, submit the required documents and complete the fee formalities.
